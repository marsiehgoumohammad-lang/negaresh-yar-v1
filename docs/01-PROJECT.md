# Negaresh Yar V1
## Product Definition Document

---

# Project Overview

Negaresh Yar is a premium digital service platform focused on professional document writing, legal document preparation, online administrative services, and AI-powered judicial document interpretation.

The V1 release is designed as a fast-launch commercial product, not a prototype.

The goal is to establish a professional online presence, validate customer demand, and create a strong foundation for future expansion.

---

# Brand Name

Negaresh Yar

Persian Brand:
نگارش یار

Brand Description:

Professional document writing, legal writing services, and online digital assistance.

---

# Product Vision

Build a trusted digital assistant platform that helps people prepare professional documents and understand complex legal documents through modern technology.

The product must feel like a serious technology company, not a small online tool.

---

# V1 Business Goal

The first version must:

- Generate customer inquiries.
- Provide AI-powered judicial document interpretation.
- Publish high-quality SEO content.
- Provide simple invoice generation.
- Create a foundation for future enterprise features.

---

# Target Users

Primary users:

- Individuals needing official letters.
- People needing legal document assistance.
- Users who receive court documents and need simple explanations.
- Customers looking for online administrative services.

---

# Core Product Principles

## Simplicity

Only essential features are included.

No unnecessary complexity.

---

## Premium Experience

The website must look and feel like a professional commercial brand.

Avoid generic website patterns.

---

## Scalability

The architecture must allow future expansion without rewriting the entire system.

---

## Human First Design

The website must communicate trust, professionalism, and reliability within seconds.

---

# Technology Stack

Backend:

Laravel 13

Frontend:

Blade
Tailwind CSS 4

Database:

MySQL

Google Gemini API

Linux Production Server

---

# Development Rules

The system must not contain:

- Artificial intelligence branding elements.
- Generic AI-generated design patterns.
- Template-based layouts.
- Placeholder content.
- Fake testimonials.
- Stock-style sections.

Every section must have a real business purpose.

---

# V1 Release Definition

V1 is considered complete when:

- The public website is live.
- AI document interpretation works.
- Social contact channels work.
- Articles can be created and published.
- SEO foundations are active.
- Invoices can be generated.
