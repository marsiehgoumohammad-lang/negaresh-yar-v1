# Scope Definition

This document defines exactly what belongs to V1.

The purpose is preventing unnecessary complexity and keeping the project launch-focused.

---

# Included in V1

## Website

- Homepage
- Services page
- AI interpretation page
- Request/contact page
- Articles section
- Contact page

---

## AI Features

- Gemini-powered judicial interpretation
- AI-assisted article creation
- Article scoring workflow

---

## Administration

- Article management
- SEO management
- Social link management
- Invoice management
- Website settings

---

## SEO

- Technical SEO foundation
- Search engine indexing preparation
- Structured metadata

---

# Not Included

## User Management

Not included:

- Registration
- Login
- User profiles
- User dashboards

---

## Customer Management

Not included:

- Customer database
- Customer history
- Customer accounts

---

## Communication Systems

Not included:

- Internal chat
- Messaging
- Support tickets

---

## Financial Systems

Not included:

- Online payments
- Accounting
- Subscription system

---

# Scope Rules

Any new idea must answer:

"Is this required for launching V1?"

If the answer is no:

The feature moves to V2.

---

# Development Priority

Priority order:

1. Business functionality
2. User experience
3. SEO
4. Visual refinement
5. Future scalability
