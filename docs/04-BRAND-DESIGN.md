# Negaresh Yar V1
## Brand and Design Guidelines

---

# Brand Position

Negaresh Yar must be perceived as:

- Professional
- Reliable
- Modern
- Premium
- Human-centered

The website must look like a serious technology company.

---

# Design Direction

The design must be:

- Custom
- Minimal
- Elegant
- Professional
- Trust-oriented

---

# Forbidden Design Patterns

Do not use:

- AI-generated website styles
- Generic SaaS templates
- ThemeForest layouts
- Excessive gradients
- Purple/blue AI gradients
- Robot illustrations
- Brain AI symbols
- Neon effects
- Fake futuristic interfaces

---

# Typography Rules

Avoid:

- Common AI-generated font choices
- Overused startup fonts
- Decorative fonts

Typography must:

- Improve readability
- Feel professional
- Match a legal/business brand

---

# UI Principles

Every component must have a purpose.

Avoid:

- unnecessary cards
- excessive animations
- visual noise
- decorative sections without business value

---

# Mobile Experience

Mobile is the primary design target.

Requirements:

- Fast loading
- Clear hierarchy
- Large touch targets
- Simple navigation

---

# Brand Trust Requirements

Within seconds users should understand:

- What Negaresh Yar does
- Why they should trust it
- How to contact the business

---

# Original Design Requirement

The final product must not look like an AI-generated website.

It must feel designed by an experienced product design team.
